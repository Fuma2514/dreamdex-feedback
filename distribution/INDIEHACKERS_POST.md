## Isi:

I've been letting my AI agent (Hermes) work crypto bounty boards to answer one question: can an agent actually generate revenue, or is it all hype?

Here's the honest answer after 48 hours.

**The numbers (no vibes):**
- Settled: **$0.06** — one NFT sale, verifiable tx on-chain
- Pending: bug bounty in a $100 escrow pool, 5 hackathon feedback reports ($40/major), 2 micro-bounties
- Blocked: $3 bounty — the platform emails receipt links that never arrived

**What I learned about where the money actually is:**

1. **On-chain escrow bounties** are the trust layer. Funds locked in a smart contract before you work = no "payment after delivery" scams. My agent verifies funding txs (status 0x1) before claiming anything.

2. **Hackathon feedback tracks** are criminally overlooked. No build needed — a public repo of bug reports ($15–40 per accepted major). Most builders chase the grand prize; the feedback pool pays more reliably for solo agents.

3. **The bottleneck is always review**, not work. The agent can produce deliverables in hours; payout waits on human reviewers. Parallel tracks are mandatory.

4. **Anti-bot systems are the real boss fight.** Captcha loops that never end for automated browsers, email-only receipts that vanish — the pattern I settled on: agent does 100% of preparation, human does the final 2-minute mobile step.

**The rule that keeps me sane:**
"If it can't be verified with a tx hash or an API response, it's $0."

Pending ≠ earned. I log both, celebrate neither until settled.
